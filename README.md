# Synthesis of Gold Nanorods for Biomedical Applications

## Overview

This experimental research project focuses on the synthesis and characterization of gold nanorods (AuNRs) using the seed-mediated growth method for potential applications in Photothermal Therapy (PTT). The main objective is to study how different synthesis conditions affect nanorod formation, optical properties and colloidal stability.

## Methodology

A total of 16 experimental batches were prepared, including one control batch without silver nitrate (AgNO₃) and 15 batches with varying concentrations of AgNO₃, CTAB, ascorbic acid, gold precursor and seed solution.

The prepared samples were characterized using:

- **UV-Vis Spectroscopy:** Analysis of transverse and longitudinal plasmon absorption peaks.
- **IGOR Pro:** Gaussian curve fitting, spectral deconvolution and peak-height ratio (A₁/A₂) analysis.
- **FE-TEM:** Examination of particle shape and nanorod formation.
- **DLS and Zeta Potential:** Evaluation of hydrodynamic size and colloidal stability.

## Key Results

- AgNO₃-assisted synthesis showed improved nanorod formation compared to synthesis without AgNO₃.
- Sample 4 achieved the lowest A₁/A₂ ratio of **0.233**, indicating promising optical characteristics.
- Sample 6 exhibited the highest longitudinal peak wavelength of **663.4 nm**, with nanorod formation confirmed through FE-TEM.
- Sample 2 exhibited a zeta potential of **+40.75 mV**.

## Future Scope

Further optimization of reagent concentrations to improve nanorod formation and shift longitudinal plasmon absorption towards the near-infrared region (750–900 nm) for potential photothermal therapy applications.
